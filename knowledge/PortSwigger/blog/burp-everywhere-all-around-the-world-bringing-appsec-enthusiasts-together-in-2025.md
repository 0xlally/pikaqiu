Security is a team sport. Whether you're a pentester, bug bounty hunter, student, or just love breaking (and fixing) things, our field thrives on shared knowledge, collaboration, and support.

We want to make it even easier for AppSec professionals like you to connect—whether that’s through local meetups, online discussions, or major events. We’re here to help bring people together, share insights, and strengthen the community that makes web security better for everyone.

That’s why in 2025, we’re doubling down on our mission to bring AppSec professionals together across the globe - whether that’s online or in person - and we’d love for you to be a part of it!

Burp on Tour: Where We’ll Be in 2025

We love online events, but nothing beats meeting the community in person. That’s why we’re hitting the road in 2025, sponsoring and attending some of the best security conferences and hackathons out there.

Here are just a few places you can find us:

BSides Exeter (April 26)

A grassroots infosec conference packed with innovation and collaboration, right in England’s South West.

Hacking APIs Con NYC (May 15)

Burp’s coming to the Big Apple for a new dedicated API testing conference.

SteelCon (July 11-13)

One of the UK’s top security conferences, bringing together pros, researchers, and hackers in Sheffield.

RomHack (Sept 23-26)

A top-tier technical security conference in the beautiful city of Rome. (Come for the research, stay for the pasta!).

University of Manchester Tech Society Hackathon (April 6-7)

A student-led hackathon shaping the next generation of AppSec pros.

Oh, and we’re also planning something special for Black Hat and DEF CON

this year. Watch this space!

If you’re attending any of these events, let us know! We’d love to say hi—and hey, there might even be a free Burp t-shirt in it for you.
