At PortSwigger, we’re always looking for ways to enable the world to secure the web, and today we’re excited to take that mission a step further.

We’re pleased to announce a new collaboration bringing together Burp Suite Professional - the industry-standard toolkit for manual web security testing - and Intigriti, a leading bug bounty and vulnerability disclosure platform connecting organizations with a global community of ethical hackers. Together, we’re empowering security teams and researchers to uncover real-world vulnerabilities more effectively than ever.

Why are we collaborating with Intigriti?

Our collaboration with Intigriti puts Burp Suite Professional in the hands of proven bug bounty hunters and rewarding the people who consistently deliver high-impact web findings. The goal is simple: help hunters work faster, go deeper, and surface meaningful vulnerabilities that make the web safer for everyone.

Rewards for Top Bug Hunters

Top-performing researchers on the Intigriti bug bounty platform can now earn a free 6-month Burp Suite Professional license* by achieving 400 reputation points. This supports researchers by providing a critical, professional-grade tool that significantly boosts efficiency. Burp Suite Professional offers advanced features including deep and comprehensive scanning allowing more productive workflows.

Equipping bug hunters with Burp Suite Professional helps them uncover complex vulnerabilities faster and report them with greater clarity, strengthening the collaboration between bug hunters and companies on Intigriti. This initiative underscores our commitment to the global bug hunting community and excellence in web security testing.

*Bug bounty hunters/hackers will be eligible for a free 6-month Burp Suite license one time per year.

How To Get Involved

If you’re already active on Intigriti, start tracking your points today and once you hit 400 reputation points, you’ll be eligible for your free Burp Suite Pro license. And if you’re new to Intigriti or curious about how this all works, head over to the Intigriti platform and join the crowd of bug bounty hunters helping make the digital ecosystem safer for everyone.

Looking Ahead

This collaboration is just the beginning. Over the course of the year, PortSwigger and Intigriti will collaborate on community events, challenges, and opportunities that bring researchers together and celebrate great work in the bug bounty space. Whether it’s shared workshops, hacking sessions, or community-driven programming, there’s lots to look forward to, so watch this space!
