Application security teams are under pressure. With expanding application estates, growing API usage, and faster release cycles, many teams struggle to keep up. Backlogs grow, releases are delayed, and sometimes apps ship without proper testing.

Earlier this year we surveyed AppSec leaders across several industries and regions, and more than half of them told us they were struggling with the complexity of modern applications.

Yet one thing remains constant: security professionals trust Burp Suite Professional for its accuracy, depth, and flexibility. The question is—how can teams scale that same level of trusted testing across hundreds of apps and APIs, without slowing down workflows?

Watch the webinar below or read on to see how Burp Suite DAST helps answer that challenge:

Enterprise-scale security from the makers of Burp Suite

Burp Suite DAST is powered by the same proprietary scanning engine as Burp Suite Professional, built on nearly two decades of leading application security research. It brings the robustness and accuracy that security professionals already trust in, and scales it up to enterprise level.

Because Burp Suite DAST operates within the same ecosystem that your penetration testers already use, adoption is seamless. There’s no need to relearn tooling or reinvent workflows. AppSec teams benefit from consistent and familiar scan results, as well as reliable remediation advice that makes scaling coverage across hundreds of apps and APIs both efficient and effective.

Enterprise-grade accuracy: Powered by the same engine trusted by tens of thousands of security professionals.

Flexible deployment: SaaS or self-hosted, with Kubernetes support for auto-scaling your scanning infrastructure according to demand.

API and web app coverage: Scan applications and APIs with a single, proven engine.

Seamless integrations: Connect easily with your existing security systems, including VMS, ASPM, SIEM and ticketing platforms like Jira, GitLab and Trello.

Customizable scanning: Import and reuse your Burp Suite Professional scan configurations.

Extensible: Add your team's favorite extensions and custom scan checks from Burp Suite Professional to keep up with the latest threats.

CI/CD ready: Integrates seamlessly with your existing CI/CD pipelines, with platform-agnostic CI-driven scanning.

How Burp Suite DAST Fits Your Workflow

Burp Suite DAST complements your manual testing by handling the repetitive, automated tasks. This frees up your skilled testers to focus on high-impact security challenges. With features like role-based access control, SIEM integrations, and rich reporting, it supports both technical workflows and high-level business needs.
