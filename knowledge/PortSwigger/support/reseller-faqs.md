Frequently asked questions

Quotation and pricing

How do I get a quote?

Generate a new Burp Suite Professional reseller quote here.

Contact us for a Burp Suite DAST quote.

How long is my quote valid for?

All Burp Suite quotations are valid for 90 days.

What payment methods are accepted?

Our payment methods are credit card, PayPal, wire transfer and Bitcoin. We always require payment in advance of delivery.

If you are sending a wire transfer, please use the order number as the reference.

Full details of payment options can be found here.

What discount do I get as a reseller?

Unfortunately, we are unable to offer any discount based on the number of licenses or subscription length.

Compared to other tools, Burp is priced extremely competitively.

We prefer to keep our headline price low and charge the same to everyone.

Can I purchase a multi-year subscription?

Yes, we offer 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 year subscription terms.

Order management

My customer wants to add more users to their license, how can I do this?

Please email hello@portswigger.net with the end user (license holder) email address, current license expiry date and how many users they would like to add to their subscription and we can send you a quote.

What do I need to provide to get a renewal quote?

So that we can send you a renewal quote, you need to provide us with either the end user's (license holder) email address, last year's PortSwigger order number or the first 5 characters of the license key.

How can I get a renewal quote for my customer?

We will send you renewal reminder emails 120, 90, 60 and 30 days prior to your customer's license expiry.

The link to your renewal quote is contained in the renewal reminder email.

If we have not sent you a renewal quote, please contact us with the end user's (license holder) email address or last year's PortSwigger order number so we can provide this to you.

How do I get an invoice?

If you already have a quote, click on the orange "view invoice" button on your quote.

Where is my receipt or invoice?

Your receipt & invoice will be sent to the email address listed on the order once payment has been made and the order completed.

How do I amend my quote or invoice?

You can amend your quote or invoices by using the orange "Edit" buttons.

If you need to amend the end user email address (who the license will be registered to) then you will need to contact us.

Licensing and product information

What's the difference between Burp Suite Professional & DAST?

Burp Suite Professional is our desktop-based manual

penetration testing web security toolkit. Pricing for Pro is based on a per-user basis.

Burp Suite DAST is our automated

DAST scanner that scales with an organization's growing security testing requirements.

Users can manage and schedule recurring (or on-demand) scans against their

portfolio of web applications/sites via an intuitive and easy-to-use web interface.

Burp Suite Professional and Burp Suite DAST are designed to compliment

each other and be used together. Using the two together ultimately results in

comprehensive security testing, enhancing an organization's ability to identify and

remediate vulnerabilities effectively.

We also have a feature breakdown of each application on

our

product comparison page.

Where is the license key?

License keys can be found and managed through the end user's (License Holder) PortSwigger account. License keys are not sent to the reseller.
