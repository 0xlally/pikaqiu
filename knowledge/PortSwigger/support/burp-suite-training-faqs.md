Burp Suite Training FAQ's

Do you provide Burp Suite training?

Face to face training is provided through our training partners around the world and includes both public training and training tailored to the requirements of an individual organization.

Are there any self-study training options available?

As well as face to face training, there are a number of self-study training options for users who would prefer this option.

Can you give me the dates and locations of forthcoming public training?

All forthcoming public training dates and locations can be found on our training page.

I would like to purchase a training license, is this possible?

You can purchase a short-term training license, valid for each student attending a course. If you are a training business wishing to purchase a training license, please email us with the number of students you require the license for and we will be able to provide you with a quotation.

Are there training licenses for educational institutions?

Training licenses are available for educational institutions, specifically for the web application security modules of courses. Please email us with details of the educational establishment, the course and module that the licenses are required for, and the number of students enrolled on the course.

How do I become an authorised Burp Suite trainer?

We work with a select number of training providers to offer good quality training courses on Burp Suite. If you are a trainer and would like to offer a Burp course to our users, you can email us for more information on the training requirements.
