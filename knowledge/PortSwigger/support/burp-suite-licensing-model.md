Burp Suite subscription model

What's included in a Burp Suite subscription?

A Burp Suite subscription includes software licenses; access to technical and customer support services; and new versions of the software as they are released.

We offer annual subscriptions, and many customers choose to subscribe for multiple years.

How do I renew my subscription?

To renew your subscription, we will take a payment 14 days before the expiry date from the payment card saved on your account.

If you have not yet saved a payment card under your account, you can renew your subscription from the link provided in the reminder emails.

If you are a reseller renewing a subscription on behalf of a customer please email us.

I have more than one license key and I would like to merge these into a single key or to align all the expiry dates so they co-term.

Please email us so that we can merge or co-term your licenses. There is no charge for this, other than the cost of extending expiry dates where needed to align expiry dates (this is calculated pro rata, based on our annual license fee and the number of days involved in any extension).

Burp Suite Professional subscriptions

How many people can use a single Burp Suite Professional subscription?

Everyone who uses Burp Suite Professional needs to have a subscription.

This number can be specified during the ordering process and is displayed within the caption in the product.

You can not share a single Burp Suite Professional subscription between multiple users, even if only one person uses it at a time.

For the avoidance of doubt, "users" means actual individual people, not concurrent users or installations or anything else.

Can I install Burp Suite Professional software on more than one computer?

Yes. We provide the ability to activate Burp Suite on more than one computer.

Each installation of Burp Suite Professional needs to be activated before it will operate. We recognize that users may need to install Burp on more than one computer. Within reason, additional activations are allowed provided that you abide by the license terms. We reserve the right to limit the number of activations allowed per subscription, and to prevent further activations if this limit is exceeded.

Can I buy subscriptions for multiple users?

You can subscribe your entire team or company to Burp Suite Professional during the ordering process, by specifying how many users you have.

You can not share your Burp Suite Professional subscription between multiple users, even if only one person uses it at a time.

Burp Suite DAST subscriptions

What comes with a Burp Suite DAST subscription?

Burp Suite DAST subscriptions include software licenses; access to technical and customer support services; and new versions of the software as they are released.

We offer annual subscriptions, and many customers choose to subscribe for multiple years.

Unlike Burp Suite Professional, Burp Suite DAST can be used by unlimited users.

What will my Burp Suite DAST subscription cost?

The cost of a Burp Suite DAST subscription changes depending on how many websites you need to secure. You can find detailed pricing for Burp Suite DAST here.

Can I upgrade from Burp Suite Professional subscription to an DAST subscription?

No, Burp Suite Professional and Burp Suite DAST are two different products, so there is no upgrade path.
