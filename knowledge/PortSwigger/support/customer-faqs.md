Frequently asked questions

For additional support and technical information, please see our support and documentation. If your question isn't answered within our

documentation, or by any of the below responses, please contact us.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us for a Burp Suite DAST quote.

Generate a Burp Suite Professional quote here.

I already have a subscription, how do I renew?

Save a credit card to your account, and turn on auto-renew. This is the easiest way to ensure you have continued access to Burp, as the license

will automatically renew at the end of your subscription.

Alternatively, you're able to renew your subscription via your PortSwigger account. Click "Manage Subscription" then "Renew" option next to the

relevant subscription in your account dashboard and either pay or generate a quotation or invoice.

Can I send you a Purchase Order?

You do not need to send us your PO as we require prepayment for all orders.

If you need to add the PO Number for your own procurement processes, just click "View invoice" on your quotation and then "Add Purchase Order details" to add your PO number to the invoice.

How do I purchase via a Reseller?

If you're purchasing a new license, find a Reseller here and let them know the details of who you would like the license to be registered to.

Looking to renew via a Reseller? Provide your reseller with either the previous PortSwigger Order Number or the Email address of the End-User currently associated with the subscription so they

can contact us for a renewal quotation.

How do I add more users to my Burp Suite Professional license?

You can add new users to your existing Burp Suite Professional license from your PortSwigger account. Find your subscription and click on the "Increase users" option from the Manage Subscription

dropdown box and follow the prompts to the payment screen. Please note the cost is calculated pro-rata based on the number of days remaining on the license.

Looking to add users via a reseller? Provide your reseller with either the previous PortSwigger Order Number or the Email address of the End-User currently associated with the subscription and how

many users you would like to add so they can contact us for a quotation.

How many people can use a Burp Suite Professional subscription?

Each individual using Burp Suite Professional must have their own subscription. Subscriptions can't be shared between individuals, even if only one person uses Burp Suite Professional at a time.

In this context, individuals means actual people, not concurrent users or installations.

The number of users is specified during purchase, and you can add users anytime through your PortSwigger account, under Manage subscription > Increase users.

Can I install Burp Suite Professional on more than one computer?

Yes, you can install Burp Suite Professional on multiple devices. Each device must be activated separately. Activations are allowed within reasonable limits, provided you follow the license terms, such as the number of users who are licensed to use the software.

We reserve the right to limit the number of activations per subscription and restrict further activations if this limit is exceeded.

When activating Burp Suite Professional, I receive a message that says No more activations

Each installation of Burp Suite Professional requires activation. If you see a message saying No more activations or Activation failed, it means you've reached the activation limit.

In this situation, please email us at hello@portswigger.net for support.

How do I remove Burp Suite Professional software?

To remove Burp Suite Professional from your computer:

Click the top-level Help menu.

Select Clean Burp from computer. The Clean Burp from computer dialog opens.

Choose the items you want to remove.

This process doesn't reduce the number of recorded license activations.

Are there any shipping or other costs?

As we deliver the license electronically there are no shipping costs.

Can I purchase a multi-year subscription?

Yes, we offer 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 year subscription terms - you can create as many quotes as you need.

How do I set my Burp Suite Professional subscription to automatically renew?

Log into your PortSwigger account and navigate to the "Your Subscription" page and switch on the auto-renewal toggle. You will also need to save your card details in the "Personal details" section.

How do I transfer my license to another user?

To transfer your license to another account, send your request to hello@portswigger.net specifying the new end user's details (name and email address). Once the transfer is complete, the new end user

will receive a confirmation email.

Please note, the new end user must have their own PortSwigger account first.

Where is my license key?

You can access your license keys through your PortSwigger account.

How can I retrieve my PortSwigger account password?

You can use the "Forgot your password?" link from the user account login page at any point to request a new password.

Why am I being charged US sales tax?

Since December 2024, we've begun collecting US sales tax in applicable states.

How is US sales tax calculated?

Sales tax is calculated on a state level, based on the address you entered. We use Vertex, a third-party service, to provide the relevant rate and calculation at the time you place your order.
