Better together

Our culture is our most important superpower,

and our biggest differentiator as an

organisation. We're proud of our culture, and

fiercely protective of it.

Why we’re better together

A key aspect of our culture is that we are better

together. Our teams come together in our

PortSwigger offices

every day because we believe that by being

together, we can:

Achieve the incredible.

Support each other.

Trust and be open.

Have fun!

Why our swiggers love coming to work

Hear directly from our amazing Swiggers to learn about what

they get up to, and why they love coming to

work every day.

Why we work together in the office

Working together wherever possible,

enables:

Pairing and collaboration.

Spontaneous discussion.

Open sharing of ideas.

Group dialogue and participation in

decision-making.

Resulting in teams that:

Achieve rapid progress.

Work in an iterative, agile way, with real-time

feedback.

Fail fast, share learnings from mistakes, and

grow together.

Foster a strong sense of belonging for the

Swiggers in them.

How we invest in being better together

In support of our Better

Together approach to work, we invest in:

People and culture

Work-life balance is highly encouraged.

Flexible hours accommodate commuting and

personal circumstances.

Swiggers are given trust and autonomy.

A strong sense of community and

belonging.

Workplace and amenities

Beautiful, open-plan office space to support

collaboration.

Breakout space to allow you to come together

with fellow Swiggers to socialise and build

connections.

Free drinks and snacks available in all

offices.

Company funded socials and opportunities to

come together over a meal (either in our offices

or outside depending upon location).

High performing teams

Strong team relationships.

Sense of belonging.

Understanding, openness, and trust.

High bandwidth communication, face to face

wherever possible.
