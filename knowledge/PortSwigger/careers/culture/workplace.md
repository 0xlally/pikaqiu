Mens sana in corpore sano

It’s great to be smart, solving interesting problems,

and creating cool things. But all this ultimately

depends on being in good mental and physical

health.

Across all our locations, we actively promote a healthy

lifestyle through our culture, ways of working, and

office environments. Our workspaces are designed to

support well-being, with sitting/standing desks

available - some Swiggers even choose to walk at their

desks using treadmills. We provide nutritious food and

snack options and encourage fitness and exercise in

various ways.

We take mental health seriously. We don't work

sweatshop hours. We avoid stress. We actively look out

for the mental wellbeing of our people and provide

appropriate intervention and support when

necessary.

Flexible working

Core working hours are 9am to 5pm. There is flexibility to

shift your day earlier or later, starting any time between

8am and 9.30am.

We are not a sweatshop, and we believe that working longer

hours is neither sustainable nor productive. We work hard

and intensely during the working day, and then we go home to

enjoy the rest of life.

We work better together, and our highly interactive,

fast-paced collaboration is both fun and a key contributor

to our success. While technology has made remote working

feasible, it cannot fully replace the high-bandwidth

conversations and primal bonding that are possible when

teams are physically together. All our people work using

laptops and can connect to our systems from anywhere. This

provides flexibility for incidental remote working when

needed; for example, to receive a delivery, let a plumber

in, or provide emergency childcare. But we expect our people

to work in our offices for the bulk of the working day and

week.Discover why we work better together
