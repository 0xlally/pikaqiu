# PortSwigger standard terms for working with recruitment agencies

Source: https://portswigger.net/careers/recruiters/standard-terms
Fetched: 2026-06-28T09:17:01.293408+00:00

Other than for graduate positions, we don't ever have a fixed salary range for an opportunity. In general, we will select the right person for an opportunity, and then consider what they

are worth to us. This is always determined individually based on the candidate's capabilities.
