About the role

Ever wanted to be part of something from the ground up? We're launching a brand-new office in central London, and we're looking for the best software engineers to help shape it. This is your chance to be part of that small, tight-knit founding team while still staying connected to our core engineering back in Knutsford.

We're after software engineers who love solving hard problems and thrive in an environment that lets them roll up their sleeves and get stuff done without all the red tape. If you've got experience at top-tier tech companies and are itching for a new challenge, this is it. You'll have the freedom to make a real impact, working alongside some of the sharpest minds in the industry.

It's not just about code, it's about building something exciting together. If that sounds like your kind of thing, we'd love to have you on board.

About you

We are looking for software engineers with a passion for building excellent software and working within a collaborative environment. We leverage agentic coding across our codebases, so experience in specific languages matters less than your ability to work effectively with these tools. Your ability to thrive in a team, share knowledge, and engage in open problem-solving discussions is key.

We welcome applicants from a range of experience levels; however, you should have at least 5 years of hands-on software engineering experience and a proven track record of leadership - whether as a senior software engineer, tech lead, or through demonstrated examples of taking on contextual leadership in dynamic, fast-paced environments.

Skills we value

Experience with languages such as Java, C#, or JavaScript - though specific language expertise is not required. We leverage agentic coding and look for engineers who can work effectively across our codebases.

Familiarity with tools like IntelliJ IDEA, Kubernetes, Docker, and AWS.

Ability to learn new technologies quickly and adapt to different programming stacks.

Strong leadership, collaboration and communication skills, with a focus on problem-solving as a team.

Desire to continually grow and evolve your technical and interpersonal skills.

What will you be doing?

Be one of the founding software engineers of our new London office, collaborating closely with engineering teams in our Knutsford HQ.

Work in small, cross-functional teams on our desktop and enterprise software products.

Practise Agile methodologies, with a focus on pair programming, TDD, high-quality code, rapid release cycles, and continuous team retrospectives.

Solve complex problems in innovative ways, collaborating closely with peers to deliver creative solutions.

Contribute to a low-bureaucracy environment that enables you to focus on meaningful, impactful work without getting bogged down by red tape.
