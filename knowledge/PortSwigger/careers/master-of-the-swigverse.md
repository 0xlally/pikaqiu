The opportunity

You will engage with a wide variety of activities, working with different teams across the entire business.

Over time, you will have an opportunity to embed with each of our four tribes: technology, growth, culture, and finance. Examples of activities might include:

Devising data-driven experiments to improve a common process, such as trial-to-purchase conversion, to drive better customer experience and outcomes.

Reviewing the competitive landscape for a specific product area, to help us evolve our product strategy.

Engaging with customers to identify opportunities to improve our product UX, learning materials, and support activities.

Analyzing data from our recruitment processes to find opportunities to improve our efficiency and accuracy.

Devising effective communications that amplify the value of our products and services, and resonate with our ideal customers.

Working on hard people problems, such as helping new joiners to overcome impostor syndrome, or embedding an open feedback culture.

Collaborating with various teams in the business and supporting them in solving challenging issues.

Analyzing our transactional and financial reporting processes to identify opportunities for more efficient automation.

You will receive close support and coaching from experienced Swiggers in each area. You will gain a powerful understanding of how a successful, high-performing business operates and evolves. You will help us to make the right

strategic decisions to drive our future success.

You will steadily develop broad and deep skills in all areas of the business. You will be set for a career specializing in any area, if that suits you. Or you can choose to stay versatile, and eventually progress into a broad

leadership role.

Eligibility

You're eligible to apply.

You might be pivoting out of the early stages of your academic career. You might be returning from a career break. Or you might have done something totally different for years, and be ready for a change.
