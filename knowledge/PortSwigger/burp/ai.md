Burp AI FAQs

Is Burp AI replacing pentesters?

No. Burp AI enhances efficiency but does not

replace human expertise.

Will AI leak my sensitive data?

No. Your data stays within PortSwigger’s secure

AI platform and is never stored by the AI service

provider or used for model training purposes.

Can I disable AI?

Yes. AI features can be fully disabled from Burp

Suite’s settings.

Can I use Burp AI offline?

Not currently, but we may explore local model

options based on demand.
