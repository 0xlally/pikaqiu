PRICING AND PLANS

Scanning Features

Catch the latest vulnerabilities with Burp Scanner - the dynamic (DAST) web vulnerability

scanner trusted by over 16,000

organizations.

Discover more potential attack surface. Burp

Scanner parses JSON or YAML API definitions - scanning any API endpoints it finds.

Set your scans to run on a daily, weekly, or

even monthly basis.

Scan privileged areas of target

applications, even if they use complex login mechanisms

like single sign-on (SSO).

Scale your scanning requirements as needed,

with our transparent subscription options.

Burp Scanner uses its embedded browser to render its target - enabling it to

navigate even complex single-page applications

(SPAs).

Point and click scanning - just a URL required,

or trigger via CI/CD.

Automated OAST was pioneered by PortSwigger, and can

identify many vulnerabilities with tremendous

accuracy.

Manage security more easily, with bulk actions

for operations like canceling scans, or

launching quick scans.

Specify crawl maximum link depth, reported

vulnerabilities, fast versus exhaustive results,

and more.

Use preset scan modes ranging from Lightweight

to Deep, or create your own custom scan

configurations.

Integration Features

Integrate with any CI/CD platform. See

vulnerabilities right in your development

environment.

Track issues with Jira, GitLab, and Trello.

Auto ticket generation, severity / confidence

level triggers, and unlimited boards.

Integrate scanning and security reporting into

your own management and orchestration

systems.

Initiate, schedule, cancel, update, and work

through your scans, to get the exact data you

need, with a GraphQL API.

Tailor Burp Scanner to your exact

requirements, by writing your own extensions,

or by downloading them from the BApp Store.

Enable users to log in easily - with a variety

of SSO options (SAML or LDAP, as well as SCIM).

Integrate with any identity provider - including

ADFS, Okta, or Active Directory.

Choose from an on-premise deployment with an

interactive installer, a Kubernetes deployment,

or a cloud-based instance.

Multi-user, role-based functionality for site

hierarchy, scan detail and reporting. Give

everyone control.

Manually integrate configurations from Burp

Suite Pro, directly into your fully automated

Enterprise environment.

Reporting Features

Every issue Burp Scanner finds comes with

actionable remediation advice from PortSwigger Research and the Web Security Academy.

Graphical dashboards allow you to view bugs by

severity or type. See security posture for all

or just part of your organization.

Get reports emailed to the right members of

your team, to tailor your communications

effectively.

Export tailored HTML reports. Include any level

of detail, severity, and confidence you

require.

View deltas and other changes to visually

represent your security posture's evolution.

Know your attack surface.

Metrics include changes by issue type and

severity. See when and where bugs were

introduced.

Organize issues by their class at the touch of

a button. Focus on the vulnerabilities you want

to fix.

Almost all features can be controlled through

an intuitive, attractive UI. This opens security

up to everyone.

Check for vulnerabilities relevant to the PCI

DSS standard and 2021 OWASP Top 10, across your

whole web portfolio.
