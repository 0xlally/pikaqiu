# Enterprise Edition 2022.9

Source: https://portswigger.net/burp/releases/enterprise-edition-2022-9
Fetched: 2026-06-28T09:16:18.489876+00:00

This release improves the onboarding process for new users. We've also added a Quicklinks tab to the Home page to give you easy access to a number of key tasks, and we've fixed some minor bugs.

Improvements to the onboarding process

We've made it much easier to get started after you install Burp Suite Enterprise Edition. We've made various improvements to the setup process and we now give you two options for your first scan:

Set up a scan of our deliberately vulnerable website, so that you're guaranteed to see an example of the scan results as quickly as possible.

Set up a scan of your own site, to see real-world results.

Other improvements

We've added a Quicklinks tab to the Home page. This enables you to easily:

Run tasks, such as scanning a new site.

Manage your team.

Add integrations with other tools, such as user management or issue-tracking platforms.
