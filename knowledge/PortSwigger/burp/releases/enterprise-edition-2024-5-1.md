# Enterprise Edition 2024.5.1

Source: https://portswigger.net/burp/releases/enterprise-edition-2024-5-1
Fetched: 2026-06-28T09:16:21.321564+00:00

This release fixes a bug that was causing licensing issues after upgrading to Burp Suite Enterprise Edition 2024.5 in Windows environments.

If your installation has been affected, please follow the below steps to resolve the issue:

Re-upload your license key when prompted.

In Burp Suite Enterprise Edition, click Settings > Updates to display the Updates page.

Click Check for updates to display a list of available updates.

Click Install now for 2024.5.1 to upgrade your installation.
