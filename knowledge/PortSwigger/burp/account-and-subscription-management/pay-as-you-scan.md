# Pay as you scan (PAYS)

Source: https://portswigger.net/burp/account-and-subscription-management/pay-as-you-scan
Fetched: 2026-06-28T09:15:28.975523+00:00

We charge an annual license fee to access the product. You will then be charged per hour of scanning, billed on a monthly basis. Scans will be charged to the nearest minute and

rounded up to the nearest cent/penny.
