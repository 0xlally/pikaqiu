European Space Agency

Burp Suite DAST at an intergovernmental organization.

Introduction

Founded in 1975, the European Space Agency (ESA) is Europe's gateway to space. With 22 member states coordinating financial and intellectual resources, the ESA undertakes programs and activities far beyond

the scope of any single European country.

The European Space Agency purchased Burp Suite DAST because the software enables its teams to

automate security scanning. This automation, says its CISO, allows its AppSec team to deliver more security where it's needed.

Use case

Burp Suite DAST is valuable to the European Space Agency, because it allows it to:

Free its AppSec team to spend time where it's most valuable.

Help its development teams to ship code more quickly.

Reduce risk by gaining greater security coverage across its web portfolio.

Shift left and integrate security into the software development lifecycle.

Key features

The top two reasons the European Space Agency chose to purchase Burp Suite DAST were:

The software's simple, scalable pricing model.

Its customizable scan configurations - which allow flexibility in security testing.

"In the next year, the European Space

Agency expects to increase its use of Burp Suite DAST."

Benefits

Security is a journey. As the European Space Agency increases its use of Burp Suite DAST, it expects to increase its ability to:

Catch critical vulnerabilities across its web portfolio.

Scan more frequently; providing faster feedback to software engineers.

"Burp Suite DAST helps the European Space Agency to reduce risk, by gaining greater security coverage across its web portfolio."
