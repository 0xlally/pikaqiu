This extension provides control over the window title, allowing you to customize how the application appears in your

taskbar and window manager. It adds a Title menu to the menu bar for managing title display options.

Features

Hide or show the license suffix that appears in the window title

Set a custom window title through a dialog prompt

Reset to the original default title

View the current window title directly from the Title menu

Usage

After loading the extension, a Title menu will appear in the menu bar

Select "Hide 'licensed to' suffix" to toggle visibility of the license information

Choose "Set Custom Title..." to enter your preferred window title

Use "Reset Title to Burp Default" to restore the original title

The current window title is displayed at the top of the Title menu
