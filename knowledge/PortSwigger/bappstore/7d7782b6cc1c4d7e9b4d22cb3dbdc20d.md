This extension allows editing of Office Open XML (OOXML) files directly in Burp Suite.

It detects multi-part requests that contain files with a relevant extensions (docx, xlsx, pptx) and content.

It is particularly useful to detecting and exploiting OOXML related XXE vulnerabilities.
