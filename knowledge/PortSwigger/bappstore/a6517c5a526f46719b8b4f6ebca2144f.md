Exporter++ enables direct upload of identified security issues to Pwndoc or export to multiple document formats.

Issues are automatically sorted by severity, confidence, and name for organized reporting.

Features

Export to multiple formats: Pwndoc, CSV, JSON, XML, Markdown, and Docx

Include full HTTP requests and responses in exported reports

Use custom templates for Markdown (Apache FreeMarker) or Docx (XDocReport with Apache Velocity)

Generate executive summaries automatically using Burp AI or provide your own

Export only your selected issues rather than all findings

Usage

In the Target tab, select the issues you want to export

Right-click and select "Extensions → Exporter++ → Send to Exporter++"

Go to the Exporter++ tab and choose your preferred export format and options

Click the Export button to complete the export process
