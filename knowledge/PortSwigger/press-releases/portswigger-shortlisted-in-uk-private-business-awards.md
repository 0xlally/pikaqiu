PortSwigger shortlisted for International Business of the Year 2018 in UK Private Business Awards

6 June 2018

PortSwigger Web Security, a leading player in the web application security market, has been shortlisted in the International Business of the Year category, at the UK Private Business Awards. The awards, now in their eighth year, are positioned to recognize the success and achievement within the vast number of private businesses and entrepreneurs in the UK.

Dafydd Stuttard, CEO of PortSwigger, said: "We are very excited to have been shortlisted for the Private Business Awards. As a company we have an amazing story to tell, we are a reasonably small team, based in the North West of England, exporting to over 130 countries across the globe. The growth of the company over the last few years, the majority of which has been through international trade, is a key part of that story, so it's really nice to be recognized in this category."

PortSwigger Web Security is a global leader in application security testing (AST). Its cutting-edge software is used by over 14,000 customers in 130 countries to help them secure their web applications.
