PortSwigger ranked in Sunday Times SME Export Track 100

25 February 2018

PortSwigger Web Security, a leading player in the web application security market, has been ranked number 68 in The Sunday Times SME Export Track 100 latest league table. The Export Track 100 ranks Britain's small and medium-sized (SME) companies with the fastest growing international sales over the last two years.

Dafydd Stuttard, CEO of PortSwigger, said: "This is really positive exposure for PortSwigger and a great platform to showcase what companies like ours are able to achieve - a relatively small, innovative company based in the North of England, exporting to over 130 countries."

PortSwigger Web Security is a global leader in application security testing (AST). Its cutting-edge software is used by over 13,000 customers in 130 countries to help them secure their web applications.

Fast Track is a leading research and events company that has created a network of the UK's top-performing private companies through its rankings in The Sunday Times, from the fastest growing to the largest.
