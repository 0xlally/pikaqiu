PortSwigger Web Security announces appointment of Phil Green as Non-Executive Director

8 November 2019

PortSwigger Web Security today announced the appointment of Phil Green as a Non-Executive Director of the Board.

Dafydd Stuttard, CEO of PortSwigger, said: "We are very excited to welcome Phil to our board. Phil has extensive experience of helping subscription and internet businesses to scale and succeed and is highly aligned with our ambitions and core values. Over the coming years, Phil's skills and advice will be invaluable in enabling us to develop lean and effective systems and processes and continue our commercial success and growth."

Phil Green said: "I was excited about PortSwigger before meeting the team. Their focus on culture, being a pioneer and preparing for scale had me hooked. After meeting Dafydd and the team I simply had to get involved, to be a part of the next growth phase, build things that haven't been done before and have an impact on so many global organizations."

PortSwigger Web Security is a global leader in application security testing (AST). Its cutting-edge software is used by over 17,000 customers in 130 countries to help them secure their web applications.

Phil Green is a finance professional with over 20 years of experience. He holds advisory roles with several high-growth technology businesses. In his executive career, Phil served as CFO at Deliveroo and held senior positions at Amazon and Groupon.
